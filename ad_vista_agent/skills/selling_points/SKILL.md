# Selling Points Skill

## Purpose

Extract the core selling points of an advertising video from its Evidence Ledger.

## Boundaries

- Cover product, brand, pain points, explicit advertising claims, and evidence-backed selling points.
- Keep video facts, advertising claims, and marketing inferences separate.
- Do not infer unsupported demographics, ingredients, price, efficacy, or objective safety.

## Output

Return structured insights with a concise claim, epistemic status, unknowns, and exact speech/OCR cluster/keyframe references.
